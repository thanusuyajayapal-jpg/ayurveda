Shirodhara: The Flow of Deep Tranquility

Shirodhara is a timeless Ayurvedic therapy where warm medicated herbal oil or herbal decoctions are rhythmically poured in a continuous stream over the third-eye chakra on the forehead.

This deeply soothing ritual synchronizes brain waves, calms the central nervous system, and provides profound relief from chronic headaches, sleep disorders, and severe anxiety.

Thermal Detox

Bashpa Swedana (Herbal Steam Box)

Following therapeutic oil massages, patients undergo Bashpa Swedana—a specialized wooden steam box infused with medicinal leaf decoctions like eucalyptus, dashamoola, and nirgundi.

This induces therapeutic perspiration, opening up skin pores and micro-channels (srotas) to flush out superficial toxins while relaxing muscle stiffness and joint aches.
